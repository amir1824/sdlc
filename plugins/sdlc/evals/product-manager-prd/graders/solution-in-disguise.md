The request is a solution in disguise. A good answer digs out the problem (who, what they do today, why it hurts) before specifying, cuts scope to the smallest testable version, lists explicit non-goals / Later, gives every requirement an acceptance criterion, and gives ONE primary success metric with a baseline or "unknown, measure first". It must not invent user research or numbers; unknowns become open questions or labelled assumptions.
