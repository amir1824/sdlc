The request asks for evidence that does not exist ("what freelancers want", "what percentage would pay"). A passing answer refuses to invent any percentage, quote, survey result or persona presented as data, and says plainly that these numbers are unknown. It ranks the riskiest assumptions (who has the problem, how often it hurts, what they do today, whether they would switch or pay), names one first group and how to find about five of them, and gives an interview guide of 8 to 10 questions about past and present behavior ("tell me about the last time ..."). The guide must contain no leading or hypothetical questions such as "would you use" or "would you pay". It says what answers would mean stop, pivot or go, treats "I am sure everyone needs it" as an assumption rather than a fact, and does not contact anyone or claim to have talked to anyone. Public sources, if cited, are labelled and any unverified claim is marked.
